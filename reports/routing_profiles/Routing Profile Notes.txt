Routing Profile Notes

Bike
 - Use level of traffic stress
 - LTS 2 is where people actually bike.
 - Weight: LTS 1 = 1
           LTS 2 = 1.1
		   LTS 3 = walking speed with 25% slower for pushing a bike. (same weight as walking weight below).
		   LTS 4 = walking speed with 25% slower for pushing a bike. (same weight as walking weight below).
 - Protected Bike Lanes: Improves LTS by one level.
 - Speed estimated as average speed over TBI. 
 - Penalize slope as outlined in memo. 


Walk
 - Use Virginia DOT Classification
 - Use their weights for weightings paths, but track unweighted travel time and distance. 
 - Slopes over 10% double the travel time (account for that in the travel time). 
 - Walk at 1.35 m/s (3 mph)
 
 - Optional: Give option to ban by changing weights to infinity. That comes later. 

 
Driving
 - shortest path based on congested time
 - Some terminal time? Pull some assumptions to add in. 
 
Transit
 - Departure time flexibility
 - Non-home end should be fixed at arrival end upon arrival and departure end upon departure
 - NHB: If there is a work end, the work end is fixed. 
 - Everything else: assume departure is fixed
 - Flexibilty: Choose a time window and choose the best travel time out of that. Allow +/- 5 minutes of desired departure/arrival.
